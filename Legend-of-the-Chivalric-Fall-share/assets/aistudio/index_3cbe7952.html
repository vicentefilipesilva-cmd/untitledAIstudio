Movement (8-Way Direction):

W A S D or Arrow Keys

* 8-way omnidirectional motion. The hero's sprite maintains the last cardinal orientation when moving diagonally.

Shield Guard (Deflect Attacks):

X or SHIFT (Button X on Handheld)

* Hold to raise the Knight's Heater Shield and deflect oncoming physical attacks with zero damage!

Physical Combat & Interactions:

SPACE or Z : Sword Slash (Button A)

E or ENTER : Talk / Interact (Button B)

* Advance dialogues by pressing [SPACE], [E], or tapping anywhere on the screen.

Sword Hone Upgrade:

* Complete Master Pierre's side-quest in Val-de-Loire to double your sword damage (1 → 2)!
